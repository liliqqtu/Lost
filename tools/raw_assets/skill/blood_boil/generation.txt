Hard stair step edges, no antialiasing, no gradients, no blur, 8-12 colors. Full bleed completely OPAQUE dark colored background edge to edge, NO transparency or white/empty margin. Central subject must fit in the middle 12 by 12 logical pixels, with background extending to every canvas edge, because an external standard inventory frame will cover the outer two pixels later. Do NOT draw any border or frame. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark. Skill: 热血重燃 / Blood Boil, fighting spirit reignites when HP is low, skill and speed surge. Single bold red heart whose top rises into THREE compact golden-orange flame tongues, heart lower tip bright crimson. The heart shape with two lobes should be visibly distinct from the flame rising above it, simple readable silhouette, deep wine-red outlines and very few ivory highlights. Only one central flaming heart symbol; no shield, fist, weapon or extra objects. Dark burgundy full background with one or two subdued ember blocks near the lower corners. Strong upward energetic motion, courageous resurgence, not gore.
